Leafpost

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Leafpost is a Chrome extension that turns web pages into e-books and sends them to your Kindle. It was built so that your reading stays private: we do not run servers that receive your content, the extension collects no analytics, and we do not sell any data.

What happens when you send a page

  1. When you click Leafpost (or use its menu or shortcut), the extension reads the current page, the text you selected, or the link you chose. This happens only on your request.
  2. The page is converted into an EPUB file inside your browser. If you allowed it, images in the article are downloaded from the websites that host them so they can be included in the book.
  3. The file is uploaded from your browser directly to Amazon's Send to Kindle service using the Amazon account you are signed in with in Chrome. Amazon's privacy notice applies to that delivery.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings (your Amazon store, whether to include images, PDF layout) are saved with Chrome's storage and, if you use Chrome sync, synced between your own browsers.
  • A history of your last 100 sends (title, page address, date and whether it succeeded) is kept locally so you can see it in the settings page. It never leaves your device.
  • Your reading list (articles you save for later) is kept in the browser's local database until you send or remove it.

Removing the extension deletes this data.

Data the extension collects

None. The extension has no user accounts, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. The developer cannot see what you send, your Amazon account, or your browsing. Buttons marked Soon only open a page on our website (see below); the extension itself sends nothing.

Our website and the Pro waitlist

  • Website analytics. This website (tubetools.online/leafpost/) uses Google Analytics to count visits, including which "coming soon" feature a visitor arrived from, and some guide pages show Google ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics and advertising cookies stay off unless you accept them.
  • Pro waitlist. If you join it, we store the email address you give, the features you picked, your optional comment and where you came from (for example "popup") in Google Forms, which Google runs for us. We use them only to email you about the launch of Leafpost Pro and the features you asked for. We rely on your consent, and we delete the list within six months after Pro launches, or earlier if you ask.
  • Your rights. Write to leafpost.dev@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your data or to withdraw your consent. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Permissions

  • Active tab and scripting: to read the page you choose to send, only when you ask.
  • Amazon sites: to upload your file to Amazon's Send to Kindle service with your session.
  • Access to all sites (optional): only if you enable it, to download article images and PDFs or pages you send from a link.
  • Storage, context menus, offscreen document: to save settings, add the right-click menu, and build the e-book in the background.

Children

Leafpost is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect information from children. Please do not join the waitlist if you are under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: leafpost.dev@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub.

Política de privacidad (resumen en español)

La extensión Leafpost no recoge datos personales: no tiene servidores que reciban tu contenido, ni cuentas, ni analítica. Cuando envías una página, se convierte en libro dentro de tu navegador y se sube directamente al servicio Send to Kindle de Amazon con tu propia sesión. Tus ajustes, tu lista de lectura y el historial de los últimos 100 envíos se guardan solo en tu dispositivo y se borran al desinstalar la extensión. Los botones marcados como «Pronto» solo abren una página de esta web.

Esta web usa Google Analytics para contar visitas (en el Espacio Económico Europeo, solo si aceptas las cookies) y algunas guías muestran anuncios de Google. Si te apuntas a la lista de espera de Pro, guardamos en Google Forms tu correo, las funciones que elijas, tu comentario y desde dónde llegaste, solo para avisarte del lanzamiento, con tu consentimiento. Los borramos como tarde seis meses después del lanzamiento, o antes si nos lo pides en leafpost.dev@gmail.com. No te apuntes si tienes menos de 16 años.
