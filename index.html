Questions

Does it replace Amazon's Send to Kindle extension?

Yes. Leafpost uses the same Amazon delivery service, adds images, covers, tables of contents and PDF support, and is actively maintained.

Where do the articles appear?

In your Kindle library under Documents, on every Kindle device and in the Kindle app for iOS and Android.

Do I need to set up my Kindle email address?

No. Just be signed in to Amazon in Chrome.

Is it free?

Yes, sending articles is free. The Pro features are free during early access, until Leafpost Pro launches in spring 2027.
