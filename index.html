Leafpost

Send any article to your Kindle in one click

Leafpost turns web pages, selections and PDFs into clean e-books with images, a cover and a table of contents, and delivers them to your Kindle through Amazon's own Send to Kindle service.

Reading-friendly e-books

Ads, menus and pop-ups removed. Images, captions, tables and code blocks kept.

Cover and contents

Every article gets its own cover and a clickable table of contents on your Kindle.

PDFs too

Send the PDF you are viewing straight to your Kindle, keeping its layout or reflowing the text.

Your way

Questions

Does it replace Amazon's Send to Kindle extension?

Yes. Leafpost uses the same Amazon delivery service, adds images, covers, tables of contents and PDF support, and is actively maintained.

Where do the articles appear?

In your Kindle library under Documents, on every Kindle device and in the Kindle app for iOS and Android.

Do I need to set up my Kindle email address?

No. Just be signed in to Amazon in Chrome.

Is it free?

Yes, sending articles is free.
